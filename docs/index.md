## What is GridFPV?

GridFPV is an app for **timing and running FPV drone races**. It keeps your list of pilots
and classes, talks to your timing hardware (or a built-in simulator), runs each heat from
start to finish, and works out the standings.

**You own it.** There is no company account to sign up for and no cloud service you have to
trust. GridFPV runs on your machine, and your race data sits there with you. At a field with
no internet, it still runs your whole day.
